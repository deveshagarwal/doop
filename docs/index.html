DOOP

A personal recovery, strain and sleep app for the Google Fitbit Air, built for iPhone.

DOOP scores each morning's recovery, each day's strain and each night's sleep against your own baselines. It reads what your band, scale and calendar already record, so there is nothing to log, and every calculation runs on your phone.

What it uses

  • Apple Health: sleep, heart rate, workouts, steps, weight and nutrition written by other apps
  • Google Health API (optional, read-only): heart rate variability, which Apple Health doesn't receive from Fitbit
  • Calendar (read-only) and local weather, to spot late meetings and hot nights

Privacy

Your health data stays on your iPhone. DOOP has no servers and no accounts. Read the privacy policy.

Source

DOOP is open source: github.com/deveshagarwal/doop. Not a medical device.
